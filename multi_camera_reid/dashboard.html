Edge-Case & Crowd Protections

1

Splitting Crowds & Subsets

If a crowd of 5 enters CCTV-1, and only 2 walk over to CCTV-2, the engine uses the Hungarian Optimal Assignment algorithm to match the 2 returning persons visually, keeping the other 3 records in standby in case they appear later.

2

Visual Consistency Gating

OSNet deep neural network extracts robust 512-dimensional appearance features. Clothing color, build, and textures are verified using cosine similarity to prevent mislabeling separate crowd members.

3

Spatio-Temporal Feasibility

Transitions are automatically validated against configured travel times between cameras. Impossible transitions (like teleportation or entering camera 2 before exiting camera 1) are gated and filtered out, with overlap tolerances for parallel-processed offline frames.
